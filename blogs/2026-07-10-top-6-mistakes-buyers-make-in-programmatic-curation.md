Curation has become an exciting performance source in programmatic advertising, but it is still ridiculously easy to get wrong. The promise is simple: better packaging, better supply alignment, and better outcomes. The reality is that too many buyers treat curation like a shortcut, when it actually works best as a disciplined operating model.
